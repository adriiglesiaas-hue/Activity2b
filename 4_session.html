4ª Session

In-ear monitors

Watch the following video and answer the questions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3xD0tpaT1Nw 

English Listening Comprehension
(about the first 4 minutes of the video)
1. Who is speaking at the beginning of the video?
2. Where is the person? (At home, outside, in a studio, etc.)
3. What is the main topic of the video?
4. Does the speaker sound happy, sad, or serious?
5. What do you see in the first scene?
6. What kind of sounds or music can you hear?
7. Does the speaker talk fast or slowly?
8. What is one word you can understand clearly?
9. What color or object do you notice in the background?
10. What do you think will happen next in the video?
